Publications

ChiMERa-Bench: Benchmark for Epitope-Specific Antibody DesignCo-author

A unified benchmark for computational antibody design, built to fix inconsistent evaluation practices across the field.

2,922antibody–antigen complexes
3generalization splits
10metrics
11models benchmarked
Contributions
  • Curated a deduplicated dataset from SAbDab with epitope and paratope annotations; the largest of its kind for antibody CDR co-design, released publicly.
  • Designed three data splits (epitope-group, antigen-fold, temporal) with cluster-level leakage prevention.
  • Built a five-category evaluation protocol: sequence, structural, interface, epitope-specificity and designability, including new epitope precision, recall and F1 measures.
  • Benchmarked 11 state-of-the-art generative models under standardized conditions. Architectural inductive bias, not model scale, drove performance.

ConTact: Contact-First Antibody CDR Design via Explicit Interface ReasoningCo-author

An encoder-decoder that splits CDR design into contact prediction and contact-gated sequence generation.

11–16%better native-contact recovery
10baselines beaten
Contributions
  • Designed a three-stage cascaded decoder combining surface complementarity fingerprinting, supervised contact prediction and contact-gated antigen feature injection.
  • Introduced a distance-biased cross-attention module and a contact-weighted loss that focus learning on binding-critical residues.
  • Reached best-in-class RMSD, DockQ and epitope F1 across all evaluation splits against baselines spanning equivariant GNN, diffusion, flow-matching and autoregressive models.
  • Ran diagnostic ablations tracing sequence-recovery limits to backbone geometry constraints.
